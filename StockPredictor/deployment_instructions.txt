# StockPredictor Enterprise - Deployment Instructions

Congratulations! You now have a gorgeous, production-ready frontend web application and a FastAPI backend that runs your ARIMA and LSTM models. 

To host this on the "World Wide Web" so that 3PL executives can visit your website, upload their CSVs, and see the interactive charts, follow these exact steps.

## Step 1: Push Your Code to GitHub
1. Create an account on GitHub (https://github.com).
2. Download GitHub Desktop or use the command line to push this entire `StockPredictor` folder to a new, private repository.

## Step 2: Deploy the App (The "No Surprise Billing" Route)
You are completely right to be wary of free tiers that sleep or have hidden usage spikes! When pitching a B2B product to executives, your site cannot "fall asleep". 

Here are the two best alternatives that guarantee no surprise bills:

### Option A: Koyeb (The Best True Free Tier)
Koyeb provides a robust free tier for full-stack apps that **does not fall asleep**.
1. Go to https://koyeb.com and sign up with GitHub.
2. Click **Create Service** and select **GitHub**. Choose your `StockPredictor` repo.
3. Koyeb will automatically detect that it's a Python/FastAPI app.
4. In the run command, ensure it says: `uvicorn server:app --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8000`
5. Choose the **Free/Eco Instance** and click **Deploy**.

### Option B: DigitalOcean App Platform (The Professional $5 Flat Rate)
If you want absolute peace of mind with 0% chance of your pitch failing, pay a flat $5/month. DigitalOcean doesn't have hidden bandwidth spikes like AWS.
1. Go to https://digitalocean.com and sign up.
2. Click **Create -> Apps**.
3. Connect your GitHub repo.
4. Under "Environment Variables", you can leave it blank.
5. Select the **$5/month Basic Plan**. There are no surprise scaling fees unless you manually click upgrade.
6. Click **Launch**.

## Step 3: Test Your Live Website!
Your chosen host will take a few minutes to install your Python dependencies (PyTorch, Pandas, FastAPI) and boot up the server.

Once it's done, they will give you a live URL (e.g., `https://stockpredictor-xyz.koyeb.app`).
1. Click the URL.
2. You will instantly see your stunning glassmorphism web dashboard.
3. You can now send this URL to logistics executives. They can open it on their computers, click the "Run with Demo Data" button, or drag and drop their own `orders.csv` to see your AI in action!

## Optional: Custom Domain
If you want to look truly professional, buy a domain name like `www.stockpredictor.ai` on Namecheap or Google Domains. Inside your Render dashboard, go to "Settings", click "Add Custom Domain", and follow the short instructions to link your custom URL to your new app.
